How lecturers get an account

  1. The librarian adds you, one at a time or from the department’s staff list, and sets the courses you teach.
  2. You get a one-time setup code from the librarian. It works once and lasts 7 days.
  3. First sign-in: staff ID and setup code, then choose your own password.
  4. After that, sign in with your staff ID and password on the normal sign-in page.

Leaving the university? The librarian switches the account off, and your reading lists are handed to another lecturer or hidden.
